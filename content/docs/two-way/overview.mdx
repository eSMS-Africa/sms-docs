Two-Way SMS lets people text your business and get answers back on the same number.
eSMS Africa runs **shared short codes** in each country and gives you your own
**keyword** on one of them. A customer texts your keyword, the message reaches your
backend on a signed webhook, and you reply from the short code with one API call.

<Callout title="The short version">
You get a keyword such as `CLINIC` on the short code `3450` in Nigeria. You tell your
customers **"Text CLINIC to 3450"**. Their messages arrive on your webhook, your replies go
out from `3450`, and once you have texted someone, their plain answers like `1`, `2` or
`YES` come back to you for the next 72 hours without the keyword.
</Callout>

Request a keyword at [sms.esmsafrica.io/two-way](https://sms.esmsafrica.io/two-way).
Nothing is charged until your request is approved, and you can build and test the whole
flow in the [Sandbox & phone simulator](/docs/two-way/sandbox) while you wait.

## How it works

```
 Customer's phone            eSMS Africa                          Your backend
 ----------------            -----------                          ------------
       | 1. "CLINIC hi" to 3450   |                                     |
       | -----------------------> |                                     |
       |                          | 2. Match: STOP/START/HELP, then     |
       |                          |    keyword, then conversation       |
       |                          |                                     |
       |                          | 3. POST message.inbound (signed)    |
       |                          | ----------------------------------> |
       |                          |                                     | 4. Your logic
       |                          | 5. POST /v1/two-way/messages        |
       |                          | <---------------------------------- |
       | 6. Reply from 3450       |                                     |
       | <----------------------- |  (opens a 72h conversation window)  |
       | 7. "2"  (no keyword)     |                                     |
       | -----------------------> | 8. Matched by conversation -> you   |
       |                          | ----------------------------------> |
```

1. A customer texts your keyword to the short code, for example `CLINIC hi` to `3450`.
2. eSMS works out who the message is for (see [How inbound is matched](#how-inbound-is-matched)).
3. eSMS posts a signed [`message.inbound` webhook](#webhooks) to your backend. It tells
   you the keyword, the code, the sender, the text and the text without your keyword.
4. Your backend decides what to do: store an answer, look something up, hand off to an agent.
5. You reply with [`POST /v1/two-way/messages`](/docs/api-reference/two-way/messages#send-a-message).
6. The reply is delivered **from the short code**, and it opens a **conversation window**
   for that phone.
7. The customer answers with just `2`.
8. Because you were the last keyword to text that phone from `3450`, the `2` comes to you.

## Shared short codes and keywords

eSMS Africa owns and operates the shared short codes. Many businesses share one code,
and each business is identified by its **keyword**.

| | |
|---|---|
| Who owns the code | eSMS Africa. You get a keyword on it - nothing to lease or maintain. |
| Your keyword | One word, 2 to 20 letters or digits, with at least one letter (for example `CLINIC`, `VOTE24`). |
| Uniqueness | One keyword belongs to one business on a code. The same word can exist on another code. |
| Case | Not case sensitive. `clinic`, `Clinic` and `CLINIC` are the same keyword. |
| Reserved words | `STOP`, `STOPALL`, `UNSUBSCRIBE`, `CANCEL`, `END`, `QUIT`, `OPTOUT`, `START`, `UNSTOP`, `SUBSCRIBE`, `RESUME`, `OPTIN`, `HELP` and `INFO` can never be keywords. Numbers alone (`1`, `12`) are reserved for menu replies. |
| What customers text | `CLINIC` or `CLINIC <message>` to the code. `clinic hi`, `Clinic: hi`, `CLINIC,hi` and `CLINIC-hi` all match. |

Every keyword comes with a copyable instruction such as **Text CLINIC to 3450**, shown
on your keyword card in the app and returned by
[`GET /v1/two-way/keywords`](/docs/api-reference/two-way/keywords#list-your-keywords).
Check a keyword is free before you request it with
[`GET /v1/two-way/keywords/check`](/docs/api-reference/two-way/keywords#check-a-keyword).

## Availability

You can **request a keyword yourself** (self-service, published prices) in
Nigeria (NG), Ghana (GH), Madagascar (MG), Kenya (KE), Uganda (UG), Zambia (ZM),
Ethiopia (ET) and Cote d'Ivoire (CI).

Every other African country is **available on request**: send a request from the app
(or the API) and our team confirms availability, pricing and the documents the
networks need before anything is charged. On-request countries show no prices - they
are quoted per request. The `availability` field of
[`GET /v1/two-way/pricing`](/docs/api-reference/two-way/keywords#get-pricing) is always
current.

A short code is national: it only reaches phone numbers in its own country. Request
one keyword per country you serve.

## How inbound is matched

Every message to a shared code is checked in this order. The first rule that applies wins.

| # | Rule | Example | Goes to | `matched_by` |
|---|------|---------|---------|--------------|
| 1 | **Compliance words.** The whole message is STOP, START or HELP (or a synonym), or `<KEYWORD> STOP/START/HELP`, or `STOP <KEYWORD>`. | `STOP`, `CLINIC STOP`, `help` | See [Opt-out](#opt-out-stop-and-start) and [HELP](#help) | `keyword` or `affinity` |
| 2 | **Keyword.** The first word is a live keyword on this code. | `CLINIC 2`, `clinic: book` | That keyword's owner | `keyword` |
| 3 | **Conversation affinity.** No keyword, but a keyword on this code sent an SMS to this phone within the window (72 hours by default). The most recent sender wins. | `1`, `YES`, `Tuesday` | The keyword that last texted the phone | `affinity` |
| 4 | **Unmatched.** None of the above. | `2` from a phone nobody texted recently | Nobody. The phone gets a free hint reply. | - |

Things to know:

- **A keyword always wins.** If a customer texts `PHARMA 1` while a conversation with
  `CLINIC` is open, the message goes to `PHARMA`.
- **Only your outbound messages open or extend the window.** Each successful send to a
  phone from the code restarts the 72-hour timer for that phone. An inbound message on its
  own does not extend it, so reply to keep the conversation going.
- **The latest sender wins.** If two keywords on the same code both texted a phone, plain
  replies go to whichever texted it last.
- **The window can differ per code.** 72 hours is the default; the window that applies to
  a conversation is returned as `affinity_expires_at` on
  [conversations](/docs/api-reference/two-way/conversations).
- **Unmatched messages are never charged to anyone.** The sender normally gets a free hint (at
  most once every 10 minutes per phone), for example *"This is 3450, a shared short code. Start
  your message with the keyword of the service you want, e.g. KEYWORD HELLO. Reply STOP to
  opt out of all messages from 3450."* The hint never names any business. Our team reviews
  unmatched messages and can pass one to the right keyword (it then arrives unbilled with
  `matched_by: "admin"`).

<Callout type="info">
Design menus so a customer can always restart with the keyword: end long flows with
*"Text CLINIC anytime to start again"*. If they come back after the window has closed, a
bare `1` is unmatched, but `CLINIC 1` always reaches you.
</Callout>

## Opt-out: STOP and START

Opt-out is handled by the platform. You never need to parse STOP yourself, but you are
told about it.

| Customer texts | Effect | Webhook events | Confirmation SMS (free) |
|----------------|--------|----------------|-------------------------|
| `CLINIC STOP` (or `STOP CLINIC`) | Opted out of **your keyword only** on this code | `message.inbound` (`action: "opted_out"`) and `two_way.opt_out` with `scope: "service"` | *You have been unsubscribed from CLINIC on 3450. Reply CLINIC START to resubscribe.* |
| `STOP` | Opted out of **every keyword** on this code | Every keyword the phone has talked to gets `message.inbound` and `two_way.opt_out` with `scope: "global"` | *You will no longer receive messages from 3450. Reply START to resubscribe.* |
| `CLINIC START` | Opted back in to your keyword | `message.inbound` (`action: "opted_in"`) and `two_way.opt_in`, `scope: "service"` | *You are subscribed to CLINIC on 3450 again. Reply CLINIC STOP to opt out.* |
| `START` | Opted back in to every keyword on the code | Every keyword the phone has talked to gets `message.inbound` and `two_way.opt_in` with `scope: "global"` | *You are subscribed to messages from 3450 again. Reply STOP to opt out.* |

STOP synonyms: `STOP`, `STOPALL`, `STOP ALL`, `UNSUBSCRIBE`, `CANCEL`, `END`, `QUIT`,
`OPTOUT`, `OPT OUT`, `OPT-OUT`. START synonyms: `START`, `UNSTOP`, `SUBSCRIBE`, `RESUME`,
`OPTIN`, `OPT IN`, `OPT-IN`. Matching ignores case and a trailing `.` or `!`.

While a phone is opted out, sending to it from your keyword is refused with
`403 recipient_opted_out`. When a phone opts out of your keyword it is also added to your
account's opt-out list, which blocks every SMS from your account to that number until
it opts back in. List who opted out with
[`GET /v1/two-way/services/{id}/opt-outs`](/docs/api-reference/two-way/keywords#list-opt-outs).
Opt-out messages are never billed.

## HELP

| Customer texts | Reply (free) |
|----------------|--------------|
| `CLINIC HELP` | Your keyword's HELP text |
| `HELP` (or `INFO`) while a conversation with you is open | Your keyword's HELP text |
| `HELP` with no open conversation | The code's generic help, which names no business |

Set your HELP text (up to 480 characters) under **Manage > HELP** in the app or with
[`PATCH /v1/two-way/services/{id}`](/docs/api-reference/two-way/keywords#update-a-keyword)
(`help_text`). Say who you are, what the service does and how to opt out. Without one, the
default is *"&lt;Company&gt;: text CLINIC to 3450. Reply CLINIC STOP to opt out."* You still get a
`message.inbound` webhook with `action: "help"`.

## Webhooks

Every message for your keyword is posted to your webhook as it arrives. Set the URL per
keyword (`webhook_url`, in the request wizard, under **Manage**, or with `PATCH`), or set an
account-wide MO URL under **Settings > Webhooks**. The keyword's URL wins.

### `message.inbound`

```json
{
  "event": "message.inbound",
  "id": "a3c5e8f0-6b1d-4c2e-9f7a-1d2e3f4a5b6c",
  "message_id": "a3c5e8f0-6b1d-4c2e-9f7a-1d2e3f4a5b6c",
  "service_id": 42,
  "keyword": "CLINIC",
  "code": "3450",
  "from": "+2348012345678",
  "to": "3450",
  "text": "CLINIC book a visit",
  "raw_text": "CLINIC book a visit",
  "body_without_keyword": "book a visit",
  "received_at": "2026-10-12T09:30:00+00:00",
  "conversation_id": 981,
  "matched_by": "keyword",
  "opt_out": false,
  "action": null,
  "segments": 1,
  "billed": true,
  "two_way": { "service_id": 42, "number": "3450", "keyword": "CLINIC", "type": "shared", "toll_free": false }
}
```

| Field | What it means |
|-------|---------------|
| `keyword` / `code` | Your keyword and the short code it lives on. |
| `from` | The customer's number in international format. Reply to this. |
| `text` | The message. If you turned on **strip keyword**, keyword matches arrive without the keyword here. |
| `raw_text` | Always exactly what the customer sent. |
| `body_without_keyword` | The message with your keyword removed (`"book a visit"`). For an affinity match it is the whole message (`"2"`). Use this for menus. |
| `conversation_id` | Stable ID of this customer's thread with your keyword on this code. Key your session state on it. |
| `matched_by` | `keyword`, `affinity` (plain reply inside the window), `dedicated` or `admin` (passed to you by our team). |
| `action` / `opt_out` | `null` for a normal message; `opted_out`, `opted_in` or `help` for compliance words (`opt_out` is `true` for STOP). |
| `billed` | `true` charged, `false` stored unbilled because your balance was too low, `null` not billable (STOP/START/HELP, passed on by our team). |

### `two_way.opt_out` and `two_way.opt_in`

```json
{
  "event": "two_way.opt_out",
  "id": "evt_5f2a9c1b7d3e4a60",
  "message_id": "a3c5e8f0-6b1d-4c2e-9f7a-1d2e3f4a5b6c",
  "service_id": 42,
  "keyword": "CLINIC",
  "code": "3450",
  "phone": "+2348012345678",
  "from": "+2348012345678",
  "scope": "service",
  "occurred_at": "2026-10-12T09:31:00+00:00",
  "conversation_id": 981,
  "two_way": { "service_id": 42, "number": "3450", "keyword": "CLINIC", "type": "shared", "toll_free": false }
}
```

`scope` is `service` when they texted `CLINIC STOP`, or `global` when they texted a bare
`STOP` to the code.

### Verify the signature

Every webhook has an `X-Webhook-Signature` header: `sha256=` followed by the hex
HMAC-SHA256 of the **raw request body**, keyed with your account signing secret (shown and
rotated under **Settings > Webhooks**). Compute it over the bytes you received, before
parsing JSON, and compare in constant time.

<Tabs items={["Node.js", "Python", "PHP", "Go", "cURL"]}>
<Tab value="Node.js">

```js
import crypto from "node:crypto";

export function verifyEsms(rawBody, header, secret) {
  const expected = "sha256=" + crypto.createHmac("sha256", secret).update(rawBody).digest("hex");
  const a = Buffer.from(header || "");
  const b = Buffer.from(expected);
  return a.length === b.length && crypto.timingSafeEqual(a, b);
}
```

</Tab>
<Tab value="Python">

```python
import hashlib
import hmac


def verify_esms(raw_body: bytes, header: str, secret: str) -> bool:
    expected = "sha256=" + hmac.new(secret.encode(), raw_body, hashlib.sha256).hexdigest()
    return hmac.compare_digest(expected, header or "")
```

</Tab>
<Tab value="PHP">

```php
<?php
function verify_esms(string $rawBody, string $header, string $secret): bool {
    $expected = "sha256=" . hash_hmac("sha256", $rawBody, $secret);
    return hash_equals($expected, $header);
}

$ok = verify_esms(file_get_contents("php://input"), $_SERVER["HTTP_X_WEBHOOK_SIGNATURE"] ?? "", getenv("ESMS_WEBHOOK_SECRET"));
```

</Tab>
<Tab value="Go">

```go
package esms

import (
	"crypto/hmac"
	"crypto/sha256"
	"encoding/hex"
)

func VerifyEsms(rawBody []byte, header, secret string) bool {
	mac := hmac.New(sha256.New, []byte(secret))
	mac.Write(rawBody)
	expected := "sha256=" + hex.EncodeToString(mac.Sum(nil))
	return hmac.Equal([]byte(expected), []byte(header))
}
```

</Tab>

### Retries

Return any `2xx` status within 15 seconds and do slow work in the background. If your
endpoint fails or times out, the same payload is retried after **1 minute, 5 minutes,
30 minutes, 2 hours and 8 hours** (six attempts in total). Retries survive restarts on
our side. De-duplicate on `id`. Every attempt, with its HTTP status and next retry time,
is listed under **Manage > Webhook log** and by
[`GET /v1/two-way/services/{id}/webhook-deliveries`](/docs/api-reference/two-way/webhooks#webhook-delivery-log).

No webhook yet? Every message is also stored in your inbox (**Manage > Inbox**) and
readable with [`GET /v1/two-way/messages`](/docs/api-reference/two-way/messages#list-messages).
You can also forward each message to an email address (`forward_email`).

## Sending from your keyword

```bash
curl -X POST "https://sms.esmsafrica.io/v1/two-way/messages" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ESMS_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"keyword": "CLINIC", "to": "+2348012345678", "text": "Reply 1 to book, 2 to talk to a nurse"}'
```

- Identify the keyword with `keyword` (add `code` only if you hold the same keyword on two
  codes) or with `service_id`.
- The message goes out **from the short code** and opens or extends the 72-hour
  conversation window, so the customer's plain replies come back to you.
- You can start a conversation (the customer has not texted you yet) or continue one.
  Only send to people who agreed to hear from you.
- The recipient must be in the short code's country, and must not have opted out.
- Up to 1,600 characters (10 segments). Each segment is billed at your outbound price.
- The keyword must be `active` or `past_due`. Use a test key to send in the
  [sandbox](/docs/two-way/sandbox) before you go live.

The older `POST /v1/two-way/services/{id}/reply` still works and behaves the same. Full
details are on the [Messages reference](/docs/api-reference/two-way/messages).

You can also turn on an **auto-reply** per keyword (up to 480 characters). It is sent from
the code to each new inbound message, at most once per phone per minute, never in answer to
STOP, START or HELP, and is charged like any other message you send.

## Billing

Two-Way SMS is priced per country in **USD** and paid from your prepaid eSMS wallet
(converted to your wallet currency at the time of each charge).

| Item | When | Price in the self-service countries |
|------|------|-------------------------------------|
| **Setup fee** | Once, when your keyword is approved | USD 100 |
| **Monthly keyword fee** | On approval, then every 30 days | USD 50 per month, VAT inclusive |
| **Inbound SMS** | Each message delivered to your keyword, per segment | The country's Basic SMS price, unless a custom two-way price is set |
| **Outbound SMS** | Each message you send from the code, per segment | The country's Basic SMS price, unless a custom two-way price is set or your account has its own price for that country |
| **STOP, START, HELP** | - | Free, and so are the platform's confirmations, HELP replies and unmatched hints |

Live per-country figures are on [esmsafrica.io/two-way-sms](https://esmsafrica.io/two-way-sms)
and from the public
[`GET /v1/two-way/pricing`](/docs/api-reference/two-way/keywords#get-pricing) endpoint.
The prices on your keyword (`fees` on the service object) are the ones you are charged.
On a toll-free code (where offered) the end user pays nothing to text you and you pay
the country's toll-free inbound price.

**Rules**

- **Nothing is charged when you request.** Setup and the first month are charged on
  approval. A rejected request costs nothing.
- **Approved but your balance is short?** The keyword shows **Awaiting payment** with the
  amount due. Top up and it activates automatically within minutes.
- **Renewals** are charged every 30 days. You get a reminder about 3 days before if your
  balance looks too low. If the fee cannot be collected the keyword becomes `past_due` and
  we retry daily.
- **Suspension.** After **7 days** past due the keyword is `suspended`: incoming messages
  are still stored (and not billed) but not sent to your webhook, and you cannot send.
  Top up and it resumes automatically with a new 30-day period.
- **Low balance on inbound.** A customer's message is never dropped. If your balance cannot
  cover an inbound message it is stored and still forwarded with `billed: false`, and you
  are notified.
- **Cancelling** stops future monthly fees and frees the keyword. Fees already charged are
  not refunded.

See your fee history with
[`GET /v1/two-way/services/{id}/billing`](/docs/api-reference/two-way/keywords#billing-history).

## Request a keyword

1. Sign in at [sms.esmsafrica.io](https://sms.esmsafrica.io) and open **Two-Way SMS**.
2. Click **Request a keyword**.
3. **Country** - pick where your customers are. Countries marked *On request* are arranged
   by our team.
4. **Short code and keyword** - pick a shared code (or let us assign one) and type your
   keyword. Availability is checked as you type, and you see exactly what customers will
   text, for example *Text CLINIC to 3450*.
5. **Billing model** - standard rated, or toll free where offered.
6. **Business** - company name, your use case with sample messages, an optional webhook
   URL, and KYC documents (PDF, PNG, JPG or WEBP up to 5 MB each, or links). Typical
   documents: certificate of incorporation or business registration, tax certificate, ID
   of the authorised representative, and a signed authorisation letter on company
   letterhead. Final requirements depend on the networks. In on-request countries the
   documents are optional at first - we tell you what is needed.
7. **Review and submit.** We review the request with the networks. Nothing is charged
   until it is approved.

Once approved, your keyword card shows the instruction to give customers and a
**Manage** panel with the inbox, conversations, opt-outs, webhook log, HELP text,
auto-reply, usage and billing. The same request can be made over the API with
[`POST /v1/two-way/services`](/docs/api-reference/two-way/keywords#request-a-keyword).

## FAQ

**Do my customers need to type the keyword every time?**
Only to start. After you text them from the code, plain replies reach you for 72 hours
from your latest message. After that, they start again with the keyword.

**What if two businesses on the same code text the same customer?**
Plain replies go to whichever texted the customer last. A message that starts with a
keyword always goes to that keyword.

**Can I message customers who have never texted me?**
Yes, `POST /v1/two-way/messages` can start a conversation, as long as the number is in the
code's country, has not opted out, and you have their consent.

**Do customers pay to text the code?**
On a standard-rated code they pay their normal SMS rate to send, and nothing to receive.
On a toll-free code (where offered) texting you is free for them.

**What happens when my balance runs out?**
Inbound messages are still stored and forwarded (`billed: false`) and you are notified.
Sends fail with `402 insufficient_balance`. A monthly fee that cannot be collected makes
the keyword `past_due`, and it is suspended after 7 days until you top up.

**Can I change my keyword?**
Request the new keyword, then cancel the old one when you have moved your customers over.
