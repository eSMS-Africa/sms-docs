Voice calls are paid from the **same USD balance** as your SMS. Sandbox calls (`esms_test_` keys)
are never charged.

## Per-minute pricing per country

Each country has a price per minute. `GET /v1/voice/pricing` (public, no key needed) lists every
country with an approved price:

```json
{
  "data": [
    {
      "country_code": "UG",
      "country": "Uganda",
      "currency": "UGX",
      "per_minute_local": 0,
      "per_minute_usd": 0,
      "billing_increment_s": 60,
      "min_billable_s": 60,
      "availability": "early_access"
    }
  ],
  "currency_note": "Calls are charged in USD from your balance."
}
```

Values above are placeholders. `availability` is `live` when the country is open for live calls
and `early_access` otherwise. The price that applies to a call is returned when you create it
(`estimate.per_minute_usd`) and stored on the call (`price.per_minute_usd`).

## Billed per started minute

- A call is billed on its answered duration, **rounded up to the next full minute** (60-second
  increments, 60 seconds minimum once answered). A 34-second call bills 1 minute; a 61-second
  call bills 2.
- **Unanswered calls are not charged** - busy, no answer, failed before answer and canceled calls
  cost nothing.
- The call record shows `duration_seconds`, `billed_seconds` and `price.charge_usd`.

## The pre-call hold

When a live call is created, eSMS **holds** the cost of the longest call it could become:

```
hold = ceil(max_duration_seconds / 60) x per-minute price
```

So a call with the default `max_duration_seconds` of 120 holds 2 minutes. If your balance cannot
cover the hold, the call is refused with `402 insufficient_balance` and nothing is dialled.

When the call ends, the hold is settled exactly once:

- the actual charge is taken and the **unused part of the hold is released** to your balance,
- a call that never connected releases the **whole** hold,
- a call whose final status cannot be confirmed with the network releases its hold after 10
  minutes and is marked `failed` with `provider_unknown`.

The hold and the release appear in your transactions. Lower `max_duration_seconds` to hold less
per call - the flow still has to fit (see [Call flows](/docs/voice/flows)).

<Callout>
In the rare case the network reports a longer call than the hold covered, the difference is
charged after the call and can take your balance slightly below zero. This is the only voice case
that can; the next top-up covers it first.
</Callout>

## Campaigns

`POST /v1/voice/campaigns/{id}/validate` returns `estimate_usd` (valid recipients x estimated
call length x per-minute price) and `per_minute_usd` before you launch. During a campaign each
call holds and settles like a single call. If your balance runs out the campaign pauses with
`pause_reason: "insufficient_balance"`; top up and resume it.

## Daily spend cap

Every account has a **daily voice spend cap** in USD (holds plus charges since 00:00 UTC). A call
that would pass it is refused with `429 daily_spend_cap_reached`; the cap resets at 00:00 UTC.
`GET /v1/voice/settings` shows `limits.daily_spend_cap_usd` and `today_spend_usd`. Ask your
account manager to change the cap.

## Usage

`GET /v1/voice/usage?days=30` returns totals, `by_day` and `by_country` (calls, answered,
completed, billed minutes, cost in USD).
