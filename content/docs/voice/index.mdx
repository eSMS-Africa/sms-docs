**eSMS Voice** lets your application place phone calls that speak text, play audio and collect
keypad input. You describe the call as a short **flow**, start it with one API call, and receive
**signed webhooks** as the call is queued, rings, is answered, collects key presses and ends.
**Voice campaigns** call a whole list with the same flow, personalised per recipient.

## What you can build

- **Voice OTP** - read a one-time code out loud, for feature phones or as an SMS fallback.
- **Notifications** - payment, fraud, outage or delivery alerts people hear, not skim.
- **Reminders** - appointments, payments and renewals with the customer's name filled in.
- **IVR menus with keypad input** - "press 1 to confirm, 2 to reschedule", with branches.
- **Voice broadcast campaigns** - upload a list, validate it, schedule within calling hours, follow every recipient.

## How it works

```
 Your backend                       eSMS Africa                           Phone
 ------------                       -----------                           -----
 POST /v1/voice/calls  ---------->  checks limits, holds the
 (Idempotency-Key)                  estimated cost, queues the call
       <----------- 202 {id: "call_..."}
                                    dials over our voice network  ------> rings
       <----- webhook call.ringing
                                    runs your flow: say / play /  <-----> answered
       <----- webhook call.answered gather / hangup
       <----- webhook call.gather   (keypad digits)
       <----- webhook call.completed  bills per started minute,
                                    releases the unused hold
```

1. **Describe the call** as a [flow](/docs/voice/flows): `say`, `play`, `gather` and `hangup` steps.
2. **Start it** with [`POST /v1/voice/calls`](/docs/api-reference/voice/calls). The
   `Idempotency-Key` header is required, so a retry never places a second call.
3. **We place the call.** eSMS Africa checks your country allow-list, limits and balance, holds
   the cost of the longest possible call, then dials.
4. **Events come back** as [signed webhooks](/docs/voice/webhooks). You can also fetch any call
   and its event history by id.

## Coverage

Starting with **Uganda**, then Kenya, Nigeria, Rwanda, Tanzania, Ghana, Zambia and South Africa.
Each country is switched on for your account when it is ready for live traffic. Your account only
calls the countries on its allow-list (deny by default); any other destination returns
`403 country_not_allowed`. `GET /v1/voice/pricing` lists the countries with an approved price and
whether each is `live` or `early_access`.

## Pricing

Per-minute pricing per country, shown before you launch - early access pricing on request.
Calls are billed per started minute from the same USD balance you use for SMS. See
[Billing](/docs/voice/billing).

## Authentication

Use the same API keys as the SMS API (`Authorization: Bearer esms_live_...`), with the
**`voice`** scope. Read-only endpoints also accept the `query` scope. Every endpoint is available
under both `https://sms.esmsafrica.io/v1/...` and `https://sms.esmsafrica.io/api/...`.

## Sandbox

Calls made with an `esms_test_` API key are **simulated**: no real call is placed, nothing is
charged, `livemode` is `false`, and the call still moves through the normal states and fires your
webhooks. The sandbox works even while live voice is not enabled on your account.

The **last four digits** of `to` choose the outcome:

| `to` ends in | Simulated outcome |
|---|---|
| `0000` | Rings, not answered - `no_answer` |
| `0001` | Line busy - `busy` |
| `0002` | Call fails - `failed` |
| `0003` | Answered, presses `1` at the first `gather`, then `completed` |
| anything else | Answered and `completed` after about 35 seconds |

`GET /v1/voice/settings` returns the same table under `sandbox.magic_numbers`.
